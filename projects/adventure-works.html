Project 01 · Power BI dashboard

Revenue more than tripled year over year, and returns held at 2.2%.

I turned 9 raw CSV tables for a bike retailer into an interactive Power BI report. Leadership can see what they sold, what they kept and what's coming back, all on one screen, then drill into products, regions and customers.

Course project Built for Maven Analytics' Microsoft Power BI Desktop for Business Intelligence (Udemy), completed Dec 2024. Adventure Works is a fictional sample company.

Four pages, built for different audiences

Executives get the summary above. Managers and analysts drill into geography, products and customers.

Problem

Sales, returns, products, customers and territories sat in 9 separate CSV files. Leadership had no single view of performance, profitability or what was being sent back.

What I did

  • Power Query: cleaned, typed and shaped each table, appended three years of sales and built a calendar table
  • Data model: a snowflake schema with sales and returns fact tables and product, category, customer, territory and date lookups
  • DAX: a measures table for revenue, profit, return rate, targets and time intelligence (previous month, YTD)
  • Design: bookmarks, page navigation, slicers and edited visual interactions

What it shows

  • Revenue accelerated sharply from mid-2021
  • Accessories drive the most orders (17.0K), ahead of Bikes (13.9K)
  • Helmets have the highest return rates in the top 10 products (up to 3.3%), so they're worth a quality or sizing review
