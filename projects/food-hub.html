Problem

A food delivery aggregator wanted to understand demand across its restaurants and customers so it could improve the customer experience and grow revenue.

What I did

  • Cleaned and profiled the order data in Python (pandas)
  • Ran univariate analysis: histograms, box plots, count plots
  • Ran multivariate analysis: cost, cuisine, rating, and prep and delivery times
  • Modeled the commission structure to estimate company revenue

Recommendation

  • Promote the top 4: Shake Shack, The Meatball Shop, Blue Ribbon Sushi and Blue Ribbon Fried Chicken
  • Push higher-value items from their menus, since orders over $20 earn a 25% commission
  • Focus on American cuisine and restaurants rated 4–5
