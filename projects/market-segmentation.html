2 in 5 customers drive 81% of spending.

A fine-foods retailer was losing revenue, struggling to win new customers, and seeing its campaigns convert poorly. I segmented 2,227 customers by purchasing behavior. The five segments that emerged are so different that a single campaign can't serve them all, so each one gets its own message and channel.

Problem

Revenue was falling, new-customer acquisition was dropping, and marketing campaigns converted poorly. The goal was to understand the customer base well enough to personalize marketing and lift all three.

What I did

  • Cleaned the data and engineered features: total spend, family size, tenure, offers accepted
  • Reduced dimensions with PCA and t-SNE
  • Compared five clustering algorithms by silhouette score and selected K-Medoids (K=5)
  • Profiled each segment and built a persona for it

Recommendation

  • Premium print for Wine Connoisseurs, the most responsive segment
  • Deal-led digital ads for Budget-Conscious Shoppers and Convenience Seekers
  • Mixed channels for Cooking Enthusiasts and Foodie Adventurers
  • Refresh with post-COVID data, since shopping habits have shifted online
